## What You Just Built

Phase 16 is done. Interview Gym now tells you exactly what to focus on:

- **Three curated paths** — FE Only, BE Only, and Full Stack (a smart blend, not just both combined)
- **Three readiness stages per path** — Pass a Phone Screen → Pass a Technical Round → Pass a System Design Round
- **Stage gating** — you must pass (or mark as understood) everything in Stage 1 before Stage 2 unlocks
- **"Mark as Understood" escape hatch** — available after 3+ failed attempts, flags as weak spot for spaced repetition
- **AI auto-evaluation** — every new lesson, challenge, or question added to the app is automatically evaluated and slotted into the right path and stage
- **Multiple saved paths** — one for each company you're interviewing with, or one for general prep
- **Dynamic daily queue** — always shows you the highest-priority items to work on right now
- **Dashboard banner** — a prominent link to today's focus when an active path exists
